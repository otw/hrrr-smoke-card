# Changelog

## v0.1.0 — Initial public release

- Configurable HRRR near-surface smoke card with Bay Area and Tahoe examples.
- 6, 12, and 24 hour forecast choices that stop at the latest matching published frame.
- Buffered playback, pause and scrub controls, and responsive Material styling.
- Configurable time zone and forecast-start offset for readable forecast windows.
- Optional animated WebP builder for use outside the card.

The default NWS clock times are derived from the image publication timestamp. Confirm exact valid times on the source images when timing is critical.
